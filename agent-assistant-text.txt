## Changes

- `rules/combat/dice_pool.gd` — added three static functions per the Issue's Scope: `roll_dice(dice_count, die_sides, rng)` (delegates to `DeterministicRng.roll_die()` once per die, in draw order; degenerate `dice_count`/`die_sides` returns an empty `PackedInt32Array` without advancing `rng`), `count_at_or_above(rolled, target)` (inclusive boundary count), and `target_modifier(bonus_count, flank_modifier, surround_modifier, extra)` (subtracts the caller-supplied flank or surround magnitude based on `Flanking.NONE`/`FLANKED`/`SURROUNDED` — never both — then adds the already-signed `extra`). No magnitude is hardcoded; `roll()`, `success_symbols()` and `count_successes()` are untouched.
- `rules/tests/dice_pool_test.gd` — added tests for all three new functions: draw count/generator advancement, same-seed reproducibility, degenerate-input handling (both zero `dice_count` and zero `die_sides`, generator untouched), inclusive boundary and empty-array/out-of-range behavior for `count_at_or_above()`, and `target_modifier()` across attack magnitudes, save magnitudes, `extra` composition, and signed (non-negative) `extra`. Registered in the suite's `run()` dispatch list and updated the file's header doc comment.
